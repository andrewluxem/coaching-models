# GROW question bank

GROW provides a path for one coaching conversation. It is a question sequence, not a script. Select only the questions that help the coachee think and make a commitment.

## Goal

Clarify what the coachee wants from the topic and from the session.

- What do you want to achieve or change?
- What would a useful outcome from this conversation be?
- What would success look like in observable terms?
- Why does this matter to you now?
- Which part of the outcome is within your control?

Do not write the goal for the coachee before hearing their answer. If the outcome belongs to the manager or a formal process, state that boundary.

## Reality

Understand the current state without turning interpretation into fact.

- What is happening now?
- What have you directly observed?
- What are you assuming or interpreting?
- What have you already tried?
- What helped, and what got in the way?
- Who else is affected, based on the information you have?
- What evidence would make the situation clearer?

Record missing incidents, dates, measures, or viewpoints as gaps. Do not create a story to make the reality section feel complete.

## Options

Help the coachee generate and compare possible actions.

- What could you try next?
- What else?
- What would you try if one current constraint changed?
- Which option is easiest to test?
- What benefit and risk comes with each option?
- What support could you request?
- What did a prior attempt teach you?

The coach may offer an option after the coachee has explored their own, but should label it as a suggestion and preserve the coachee's choice.

## Way forward

Turn exploration into an observable commitment.

- Which option will you choose?
- What is the first action?
- Who owns it?
- When will it be complete?
- What evidence will show that it happened?
- What support or feedback do you need?
- How committed are you on a scale from zero to ten?
- What would raise that commitment?
- When should we check in?
- What condition would tell us to revise the plan?

A way forward is incomplete without an owner and date. Preserve a visible slot if either is unknown.

## Selection rules

- Prefer open questions over advice framed as a question.
- Ask one question at a time.
- Follow the coachee's answer instead of forcing every question in this bank.
- Distinguish exploration from a requirement that the manager must state directly.
- End with a commitment, feedback source, and check-in date.
