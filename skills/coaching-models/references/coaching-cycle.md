# Coaching cycle

Use a cycle when development needs repeated practice, feedback, and revision rather than one conversation.

## Five stages

### 1. Pre-coaching

Align on purpose, expectations, scope, confidentiality limits, available evidence, and the distinction between coaching and a formal process. State what the coach and coachee each own.

### 2. Self-discovery and awareness

Use observation, reflection, and feedback to understand the current pattern. Keep supplied facts separate from the coachee's view and the coach's interpretation.

### 3. Goal setting and accountability

Define an observable outcome, baseline, measure, owner, due date, and review rhythm. If a field is unknown, leave it visible rather than guessing.

### 4. Action learning and execution

Choose real work that lets the coachee practice. Record what happened, what feedback was received, and what the next attempt should change. The coach can guide early practice and step back as ownership grows.

### 5. Evaluation and revision

Compare supplied results with the goal and baseline. Decide whether to continue, revise, close, or hand off the work. Do not declare success without the agreed evidence.

## Practice design

A useful practice assignment names:

1. the behavior to practice;
2. the real setting in which it will be used;
3. the owner and due date;
4. the observer or evidence source;
5. the feedback date;
6. the adjustment expected in the next attempt.

Keep practice proportionate to the development goal. Do not use an employee, customer, or consequential decision as an undisclosed experiment.

## Feedback rhythm

Use short review loops:

1. state the planned behavior;
2. complete the practice;
3. check observable evidence;
4. ask the coachee what they noticed;
5. add specific feedback;
6. choose the next adjustment and date.

Feedback should arrive close enough to the practice to be useful. A cadence without a named owner, date, or evidence source remains incomplete.

## Connect GROW and PDCA

GROW prepares the choice. PDCA tests it:

| GROW output | PDCA step | Coaching use |
|---|---|---|
| Goal and chosen way forward | Plan | Define the action and evidence |
| First practice assignment | Do | Try the behavior in real work |
| Feedback and result | Check | Compare evidence with the intended outcome |
| Next adjustment | Act | Keep, revise, or replace the approach |

Repeat only when the next cycle will answer a defined question. Repetition without new evidence is activity, not development.
